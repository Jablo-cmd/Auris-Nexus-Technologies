Web Development

Web development — from a business website to a full platform.

Auris Nexus designs and builds business websites, web applications, client and customer portals, internal business platforms and digital products — the things your customers and teams open in a browser — connected to the systems and data behind them.

Start a Project See our work

What we build

  • Business websites. Fast, credible, responsive sites built to rank and convert.
  • Web applications. Custom tools for a specific job, built around your process.
  • Client & customer portals. Self-service accounts, requests, documents and status — instead of phone and email.
  • Internal business platforms. The system your team runs the operation on, in the browser.
  • Digital products. Products you take to market, with mobile apps where they belong.

Built properly

  • Responsive and accessible from the first build, not retrofitted
  • Connected to your existing systems and data
  • Performance and SEO handled as part of the work
  • Maintainable, documented code you're not locked out of
