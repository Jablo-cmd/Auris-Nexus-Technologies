Web Development

Websites, web applications and the platforms your business runs in a browser.

The problem. The website doesn't reflect the business, or a process that should live in one web app is spread across email, spreadsheets and phone calls.

The approach. We build the front end and wire it into the systems and data behind it — fast, responsive, secure and maintainable, with the code yours to keep.

  • Business websites built to rank and convert
  • Web applications and internal business platforms
  • Client and customer portals with self-service
  • Digital products, with mobile apps where they belong

Example applications: corporate and marketing sites, customer account portals, booking and request platforms, partner dashboards, internal operations tools.

Web Design

Design that looks the part and does its job.

The problem. A site or interface looks dated, is hard to use on a phone, or looks fine but doesn't turn visitors into enquiries.

The approach. We work out the user experience first, then the interface — mobile-first, accessible and SEO-aware, aligned to your brand and built to convert.

  • Modern, responsive, mobile-first websites
  • User experience (UX) — structure, flows and content order
  • User interface (UI) and consistent visual systems
  • Conversion-focused layouts and calls to action

Example applications: website redesigns, new marketing sites, design systems, portal and dashboard interfaces, landing pages.

Mobile App Development

Apps that put your service or your operation in someone's pocket.

The problem. Customers expect an app, or field and operations staff need to capture and check information away from a desk.

The approach. We build native or cross-platform apps connected to the same data as your web platform, so both stay in step.

  • Customer-facing apps
  • Field and operations apps for staff on the move
  • Mobile companions to an existing web platform
  • Offline-aware data capture where the job needs it

Example applications: customer service apps, inspection and job-card apps, delivery and logistics apps, member and loyalty apps.

Business Systems

From spreadsheets and disconnected processes to one central platform.

The problem. A core process — HR, admissions, client management, operations — is run across spreadsheets, email and memory. Data is duplicated, nothing is current, and it doesn't scale with headcount.

The approach. We map the process and the roles involved, then design the smallest platform that puts everyone on the same information with the right permissions.

  • Role-based access and multi-team or multi-tenant structures
  • Records, workflows, approvals and status tracking in one place
  • Reporting and dashboards built in from the start
  • Integrations with the tools you already use

Example applications: HR & leave management, CRM, school & learner management, operations management, internal business platforms, management dashboards.

Business Automation

Remove the repetitive manual work between people and systems.

The problem. Staff spend hours each week moving data between systems, chasing approvals, sending the same notifications and producing the same reports by hand.

The approach. We identify the highest-volume manual steps, then automate them — with clear rules, audit trails and people still in control of the decisions that matter.

  • Workflow and approval automation
  • Automated notifications, reminders and escalations
  • Data processing, validation and transfer between systems
  • Document and process automation; system-to-system integrations

Example applications: approval routing, onboarding/offboarding, invoice and document handling, scheduled reporting, CRM–finance–ops integrations.

AI Solutions

Practical AI, applied where it removes work or improves a decision.

The problem. There's pressure to "use AI", but most tools are generic and disconnected from how your organisation actually operates.

The approach. We find the specific points in your workflows where AI earns its place — summarising, extracting, drafting, classifying, recommending — and build it into the system your team already uses.

  • Intelligent assistants trained on your processes and content
  • Document intelligence — extract, classify and route information
  • AI-assisted workflows and decision-support tools
  • AI features embedded in your existing platforms

Example applications: internal knowledge assistants, contract and form processing, triage and routing, drafting and review support, business process intelligence.

Data & Business Intelligence

Turn operational data into visibility management can act on.

The problem. The data exists — in the CRM, the finance system, the spreadsheets — but pulling a clear picture together takes days, and by then it's out of date.

The approach. We connect the sources, define the numbers that matter, and build reporting that updates itself.

  • Operational and management dashboards
  • Automated reporting and scheduled distribution
  • Analytics and trend visibility
  • Data integration across systems

Example applications: executive dashboards, sales and pipeline reporting, operations KPIs, finance and utilisation reporting, board packs.
