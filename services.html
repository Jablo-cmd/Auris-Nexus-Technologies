Technology Consulting

Align technology with the way your business actually operates.

The problem. Businesses often know something needs to improve but are unsure whether they need new software, automation, integration, better data or a broader technology change.

The approach. We analyse the business problem, map the current process, identify the highest-value opportunities and define a practical technology path before development begins.

Business Systems

From spreadsheets and disconnected processes to one central platform.

The problem. A core process — HR, admissions, client management, operations — is run across spreadsheets, email and memory. Data is duplicated, nothing is current, and it doesn't scale with headcount.

The approach. We map the process and the roles involved, then design the smallest platform that puts everyone on the same information with the right permissions.

  • Role-based access and multi-team or multi-tenant structures
  • Records, workflows, approvals and status tracking in one place
  • Reporting and dashboards built in from the start
  • Integrations with the tools you already use

Example applications: HR & leave management, CRM, school & learner management, operations management, internal business platforms, management dashboards.

Business Automation

Remove the repetitive manual work between people and systems.

The problem. Staff spend hours each week moving data between systems, chasing approvals, sending the same notifications and producing the same reports by hand.

The approach. We identify the highest-volume manual steps, then automate them — with clear rules, audit trails and people still in control of the decisions that matter.

  • Workflow and approval automation
  • Automated notifications, reminders and escalations
  • Data processing, validation and transfer between systems
  • Document and process automation; system-to-system integrations

Example applications: approval routing, onboarding/offboarding, invoice and document handling, scheduled reporting, CRM–finance–ops integrations.

Custom software FAQ

What buyers ask about custom software.

South African developers typically bill between US$25 and US$60 an hour, well below UK, US and European rates. At Auris Nexus, business systems such as CRM, HR, booking or inventory platforms start from R30,000, and larger multi-team or enterprise platforms start from R50,000. We give a firm, scoped price in rand after a short consultation. Budget 15 to 20% of the build cost each year for maintenance and support.

Buy off-the-shelf when it fits at least 80% of how you work. Build custom when you are paying for many user licences, running the business on spreadsheet workarounds, or need several systems to share data. For mid-sized organisations, custom software often breaks even against SaaS subscriptions within about three years.

In phases. We start with discovery, then deliver a first working version that covers your core workflow, and add further features in later phases. You use working software early, give feedback before the next phase, and can pause or change direction at each milestone, which keeps both cost and risk under control.
