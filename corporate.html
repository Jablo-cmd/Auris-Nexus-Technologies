Corporate and enterprise

Business Management Software for Corporate Operations

Auris Nexus builds custom business management software for established organisations in Johannesburg, Sandton and across South Africa. HR and leave, approvals, client management, multi-site operations and reporting are designed around how your departments really work, instead of bending your process to fit a generic product.

Request a CIT LMS demo Book a free consultation
The operational challenge

When approvals live in inboxes, nobody has the full picture.

Organisations grow by adding people, sites and clients faster than they add systems. Requests travel by email, records sit in personal spreadsheets, and managers rebuild the same report every month. The work gets done, but slowly, and it depends on the people who know where everything is.

  • Leave, expense and purchase approvals are tracked in email threads
  • Employee, contract and client records are spread across spreadsheets
  • Nobody can say quickly how many people are on leave or on which site
  • Contract and certification expiries are only noticed after they lapse
  • Several systems hold the same data and none of them agree
What we build for corporate teams

Systems that follow your approval paths and reporting lines.

Each module mirrors a real process in your organisation, with roles that match your reporting structure. Modules share one set of people, client and site records.

HR and leave management

Leave requests and approvals, employee records, contracts with expiry alerts and attendance, with separate views for staff, managers and HR.

Approval workflows

Purchase requests, expense claims and contract sign-off routed to the right approver, with reminders, escalation and a complete audit trail.

CRM and client management

Clients, contacts, contracts and service levels in one place, so account history stays with the business when people move on.

Multi-site operations

Teams, sites, shifts, tasks and incidents in one workspace for organisations delivering services across many client locations.

Executive dashboards

Headcount, open approvals, service levels and operational exceptions on one screen, updated as work happens rather than at month-end.

Integrations

Connections to payroll, accounting, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace where those products allow it, so information is captured only once.

Who we build for

  • HR, finance and operations departments with a process stuck in email
  • Facilities, security and cleaning companies managing staff across client sites
  • Professional services firms tracking clients, contracts and people
  • Groups running several business units on separate tools
  • Organisations replacing a system their teams have stopped using

When custom software makes sense

  • Your process is specific enough that off-the-shelf products need heavy workarounds
  • You would otherwise license and connect several separate products
  • Per-user licence costs keep rising as you grow
  • You need approvals and permissions that match your actual reporting lines
  • You want to own the system and its data outright

Proof: delivered HR and operations systems

The CIT Leave Management System, delivered for a single organisation, moved leave and HR administration from forms, email and spreadsheets to a structured approval workflow with an HR dashboard and audit log. Sebetsa, our built and tested operations platform, covers workforce, sites, compliance and stock.

How we work

Map the process before writing any software.

Every engagement opens with discovery: we sit with the people who submit, approve and report on the work, map the real process including its workarounds, and agree what the first release must do. Delivery happens in phases, so one department uses a working module before the next one is built. If you are not yet sure whether to build or buy, start with our technology consulting service.
