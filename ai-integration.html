AI Integration

AI Integration Services for real business workflows.

There's pressure to "use AI" — but generic tools rarely fit how your organisation actually works. We find the specific points in your workflows where AI earns its place and build it into the systems your team already uses. No hype, no science projects. We provide AI integration services and practical AI solutions for businesses in Johannesburg and across South Africa, from document processing to AI assistants and chatbots.

What we build

  • Intelligent assistants. Chat and search over your own processes, policies and content — so staff get answers without hunting through documents.
  • Document intelligence. Extract, classify and route information from forms, contracts, invoices and emails.
  • AI-assisted workflows. Drafting, summarising, triage and recommendations built into the tools people already use.
  • Decision support. Surface the right information at the right moment, with a human making the call.

How we keep it practical

  • We start from a specific, measurable task — not "add AI"
  • We're clear about what the model does well and where it needs a person
  • We build it into your workflow, not as a separate tool to remember
  • Your data stays under your control

AI automation FAQ

Straight answers on AI for South African businesses.

The fastest wins are repetitive, high-volume tasks: answering routine enquiries on WhatsApp, email or web chat; capturing invoices and documents into accounting systems; moving data between systems; and generating quotes and reports. Start with one process that takes staff hours every week, prove the saving, then expand.

Expect a once-off build cost plus monthly tool and AI usage fees, many of which are priced in US dollars, so budget for rand fluctuations. At Auris Nexus, workflow automation is scoped as a business system starting from R30,000, and larger AI solutions from R50,000. The test is simple: the automation should save more in staff time each month than it costs to run.

Yes, with controls in place. Decide which data may go into which AI tools, sign operator agreements with providers, keep a person reviewing sensitive outputs, and log what the system does. Auris Nexus designs automations with POPIA principles built in, rather than letting staff paste customer data into public chatbots.
