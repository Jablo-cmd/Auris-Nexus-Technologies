Where we work

Based in Sandton. Working across South Africa, and remotely beyond it.

Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a technology consulting and digital solutions company headquartered in Sandton, Johannesburg. It is our only office. We work with organisations across South Africa, and with organisations in other African countries and overseas on a remote basis.

Remote engagements

How a remote project runs

The same steps as for a client in Sandton, delivered by video call and shared documents.

01

Discovery by video call

We map the process with the people who run it, screen-sharing the spreadsheets, forms and systems they use today.

02

Written scope and roadmap

You receive the scope, phases and quote in writing, so decisions do not depend on being in the same room.

03

Phased build and demos

Each phase is demonstrated on a call and tested by your team before it goes live.

04

Hosting agreed with you

We agree where the system and its data are hosted, taking into account your users' locations and your data-protection obligations.

05

Training and handover

Training, documentation and handover sessions are run remotely and recorded where useful.

06

Support after launch

Support and further phases continue remotely, within the working-hours overlap shown above.

Data protection across borders

Auris Nexus is subject to South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), which includes conditions for transferring personal information outside South Africa. Organisations elsewhere usually have their own obligations too, for example under the EU and UK GDPR, Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, or Nigeria's Data Protection Act, 2023.

Before a build starts, we agree with you where personal information will be stored and processed and how it will be protected. This is not legal advice; involve your own legal adviser where your obligations are unclear. Our POPIA checklist for business software covers the questions to ask.
