What we believe

Technology should serve the business — not the other way around.

Too many organisations end up shaping their operations around software that was never built for them. We think it should work the other way: understand how the business actually runs, then build technology around it.

That means the right solution is often smaller and simpler than expected. It means being honest when something isn't worth building. And it means the software has to be maintainable long after launch — not a black box only one person understands.

Working with us

Questions procurement teams ask us.

An Exempted Micro Enterprise (EME) has annual turnover under R10 million. A Level 1 EME carries 135% procurement recognition, so every R100 you spend with that supplier counts as R135 towards your preferential procurement score. Auris Nexus Technologies is a B-BBEE Level 1 EME.

Freelancers cost less but carry key-person risk and rarely offer support contracts, while large agencies add account-management overhead to the price. A registered studio like Auris Nexus sits between the two: one accountable, CIPC-registered company, direct access to the developer, and ongoing support after launch.

You get rand pricing with no exchange-rate risk, the same time zone, knowledge of POPIA and local payment systems, and B-BBEE procurement recognition, and you can meet in person. Auris Nexus is based in Sandton, Johannesburg, and works with clients across South Africa and the region.
