Business Systems

One central platform for the process your business runs on.

When a core process — HR, admissions, client management, operations — lives across spreadsheets, email and memory, data gets duplicated, nothing stays current, and it doesn't scale with headcount. We design and build the platform that puts everyone on the same information.

Discuss your process See case studies

What we build

  • Records & data. The people, customers, cases or assets your process revolves around — structured, searchable, in one place.
  • Workflows & approvals. The steps your team follows, with status tracking and sign-off built in.
  • Roles & permissions. Everyone sees and does exactly what their role allows — including multi-team or multi-tenant setups.
  • Reporting. Dashboards and exports designed in from the start, not bolted on later.
  • Integrations. Connected to the tools you already use so data isn't captured twice.

Systems we've built

  • HR & leave management (see the CIT case study)
  • School administration & management (see Funda360)
  • CRM and client management platforms
  • Operations and workflow management systems
  • Internal business platforms and management dashboards

The right system is often smaller than expected

We start by mapping your process, then design the smallest platform that solves it properly. You can always add to it later.
