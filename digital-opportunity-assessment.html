Free Digital Opportunity Assessment

Find out where software would save your team time.

A free, 45-minute video call on one process that is costing your organisation time. Within 3 working days you receive a short written summary of where automation or a system would help, what it would roughly cost, and what we would do first. No obligation, and no sales deck.

Request my free assessment Chat on WhatsApp
What you receive

A written summary you can act on

Delivered within 3 working days of the call. It is yours to keep, whoever you choose to work with.

How the process runs today

A short map of the steps, people, spreadsheets and hand-offs involved, including the workarounds.

Opportunities, ranked

Where automation, a system or a simple change would help, ordered by effort and impact.

Rough cost and a first step

An indicative budget range in rand and the first thing we would do, so you can plan before committing.

Who it is for

Organisations where admin is slowing the work down

Common starting points we hear about. Bring yours.

Schools and school groups

  • Admissions, learner records and fees spread across spreadsheets
  • Reports compiled by hand for principals and governing bodies
  • Parents and staff waiting on information that should be one click away

The same approach as Funda360, our school management platform.

Engineering and field-service groups

  • Staff deployment, attendance and shifts across many sites
  • Incidents, compliance documents and assets tracked in separate files
  • Site managers sending updates by WhatsApp that never reach a system

The patterns behind Sebetsa, a workforce and operations platform we delivered for a client.

Professional firms, including law firms

  • Client intake and approvals handled by email
  • Documents, deadlines and tasks tracked in personal spreadsheets
  • Personal information held without clear access control (POPIA)

Workflow patterns like the CIT Leave Management System: structured requests, approvals and an audit log.
