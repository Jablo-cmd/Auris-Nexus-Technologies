Featured project

Funda360 — school management platform

In active development SaaS Education Custom software

Turning fragmented school administration and academic workflows into one structured, role-aware digital platform that multiple schools can run independently.

The challenge

Schools run learner records, staff information, admissions, attendance, assessments and parent communication across paper, spreadsheets and disconnected tools. Administrators have little consolidated visibility, and every new requirement means another standalone file.

The approach

Design a single multi-tenant platform that each school operates independently, with a shared data model and access that is scoped by role — administrator, finance, teacher, guardian — so people only see what their job requires.

The solution

A web application covering school administration, a learner registry, admissions and alumni, staff management, and executive dashboards, built on a multi-tenant architecture with role-based access and secure authentication, including two-factor authentication for sensitive roles.

Key capabilities

  • Multi-tenant architecture — each school is an isolated tenant on one platform
  • Role-based access control, with data and screens scoped per role
  • Learner registry, admissions and alumni management
  • Staff and employee management
  • Executive dashboards — enrolment and fee-collection summaries
  • Secure authentication with two-factor authentication for sensitive roles
  • Live system-status monitoring inside the platform

Technical approach

Funda360 is a single-page application backed by a managed PostgreSQL database. Tenant isolation and per-role access are enforced at the data layer with row-level security, so a school's data and a user's permissions are applied on every query rather than only in the interface.

React TypeScript Vite Supabase PostgreSQL Row-level security Multi-tenant SaaS

What Auris Nexus did

Product design, the multi-tenant data model, role and permission design, and full-stack engineering of the platform. Development is ongoing.

What it enables

School administration in one place, digital learner records, access that is correct by role, and a scalable base for onboarding additional schools without rebuilding the system each time.

CIT Leave Management System

Delivered HR technology Workflow automation Custom software

Replacing fragmented leave administration with a structured digital workflow — employee request, manager review, HR administration — for a single organisation.

The challenge

Employee leave and day-to-day HR administration ran on forms, email approvals and spreadsheets. Requests were easy to lose, approval steps were inconsistent, and neither managers nor HR had a reliable view of where things stood.

The approach

Map the organisation's actual leave process, then build a web system around it: a clear path from employee request to manager review to approval or decline, with HR administration and permissions layered on top.

The solution

A web-based leave and HR management system for a single organisation, covering leave requests and approval workflows, employee records, attendance, contract tracking with expiry alerts, deployment sites and clients, HR requests and communications, and reporting — with an HR administrator dashboard over the top.

Key capabilities

  • Leave requests with a defined approval workflow: employee → manager review → approve / decline
  • Role-based access — employee, posting manager and HR administrator views
  • HR administrator dashboard — headcount, employees on leave, pending approvals and contract alerts at a glance
  • Employee records, organisation structure, clients and deployment sites
  • Contract tracking with expiry and expired-contract alerts
  • Attendance, documents and HR reporting in one system
  • HR requests and a tracked HR communication channel
  • Users & access management and an audit log

Technical approach

A single-page application over a PostgreSQL database, with the approval workflow and permissions enforced in the data layer rather than only in the UI. Built for one organisation, with roles that mirror its reporting structure.

React TypeScript Tailwind CSS Supabase PostgreSQL Role-based access

What Auris Nexus did

Discovery of the existing leave process, system and workflow design, and full-stack engineering through to delivery.

What it enables

A single source of employee data, faster and more consistent leave decisions, and visibility for managers and HR that the previous email-and-spreadsheet process could not provide.

Case study

LOGIOS OS — logistics operations platform

In active development Operations software Logistics Custom software

Bringing logistics operations — scheduling, jobs, incidents, fleet, drivers, customers and compliance — into one controlled, role-aware workspace instead of separate tools and message threads.

The challenge

Logistics operators coordinate jobs, vehicles, drivers, customers and incidents across phone calls, spreadsheets and separate apps. Nothing is joined up, accountability is hard to trace, and there's no single operational picture.

The approach

Design a modular operations platform on a multi-tenant foundation: shared identity, roles and audit from day one, with each operational domain built as a module that can be switched on as it's ready.

The solution

A web application where an organisation manages scheduling, jobs, incidents, a fleet register, drivers, customers, compliance and reporting from one workspace, with role-based access and a system-status view. Dispatch, trips, deliveries and fuel & expenses are in development.

Key capabilities

  • Multi-tenant architecture — each operator is an isolated tenant with its own users
  • Role-based access — scoped to the user's role, e.g. system administrator
  • Scheduling and job management
  • Incident logging and tracking
  • Fleet register and driver management
  • Customer records
  • Compliance module and operational reporting
  • Modular design — operational domains enabled as they're delivered

Technical approach

LOGIOS is a single-page application backed by a managed PostgreSQL database. Tenant isolation and per-role access are enforced at the data layer with row-level security, so every query is scoped to the organisation and the user's permissions. The module structure keeps each operational domain independent.

React TypeScript Vite Supabase PostgreSQL Row-level security Multi-tenant SaaS

What Auris Nexus did

Product and platform design, the multi-tenant data model, role and permission design, and full-stack engineering of the workspace and its modules. Development is ongoing.

What it enables

A single operational picture for a logistics business, traceable actions through roles and auditing, and a foundation that takes on new operational domains as modules rather than new systems.

Case study

Pro Energy Solutions — corporate website

Completed Web development SEO Business website

Turning a Johannesburg solar and electrical business into a clearer, faster and more credible digital presence — with the performance work measured, not assumed.

The challenge

A solar and electrical services company in Rosettenville, Johannesburg needed a stronger online presence — one that could present its services and completed work credibly to residential and commercial customers, and be found in local search.

The approach

Design and build a fast, responsive corporate website focused on clarity, trust and conversion, on a solid technical-SEO foundation, and treat page weight as an engineering problem to be measured.

The solution

A responsive multi-page website — services, work, about, FAQs and contact — with structured page architecture, a mobile-first layout, prominent WhatsApp and quote calls-to-action, and an optimised image pipeline.

Key capabilities

  • Responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript, mobile-first
  • Structured multi-page architecture with clear conversion paths
  • Technical SEO foundation — metadata, canonical URLs, structured data, Open Graph, sitemap and robots.txt
  • Local search focus for Rosettenville, Johannesburg and Gauteng
  • Image optimisation and lazy-loading below the fold
  • Accessible markup with visible focus states

The engineering result

The in-use image set was cut by 41.8% — roughly half a megabyte — with no visible loss of quality, alongside lazy-loading and defined image dimensions to hold layout stable. This is a measured website-performance result, not a claim about the client's sales.

HTML CSS JavaScript Technical SEO Performance optimisation

What Auris Nexus did

Brand-led UI/UX design, responsive front-end build, the technical-SEO foundation, and the performance and image-optimisation work.

What it enables

A stronger, more credible brand presence, a faster experience on mobile, and a professional platform for presenting completed solar work to prospective customers.
