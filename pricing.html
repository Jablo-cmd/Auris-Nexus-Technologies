Pricing FAQ

What business software and automation cost in South Africa.

At Auris Nexus, purpose-built business systems such as CRM, HR and leave management, booking or inventory platforms start from R30,000. Larger custom and enterprise platforms, including multi-tenant systems, integrations, mobile apps and legacy modernisation, start from R50,000. The final price depends on scope: the number of users and roles, the integrations needed and how much existing data has to move. After a short conversation we scope the work and give you a firm number.

Workflow automation is scoped as a business system, starting from R30,000 for approvals, notifications, document handling and data moving between your existing tools. Automation at larger scale, or combined with AI, falls under custom and enterprise platforms from R50,000. Budget separately for any monthly subscriptions the connected tools charge.

Expect two parts: a once-off build cost and monthly running costs. AI solutions are built as custom platforms starting from R50,000. Running costs are the AI model and tool usage fees, which are usually billed per use and often priced in US dollars, so budget for rand fluctuations. We estimate the monthly usage cost before you commit, based on your real volumes.

Professional websites start from R12,500 for up to 10 responsive pages, including UI/UX design, contact forms, WhatsApp integration, Google Maps and Analytics, basic SEO and 30 days of post-launch support. A custom business website usually takes 3 to 6 weeks from discovery to launch.

Website care plans start from R950 a month (security updates, backups and basic monitoring) and from R2,500 a month for priority support and content updates. Enterprise support, monitoring and a custom SLA are quoted per engagement.
