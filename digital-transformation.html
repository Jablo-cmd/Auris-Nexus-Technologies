Digital Transformation

Digital Transformation Consulting for South African Organisations

Digital transformation is not a software purchase. It is changing how work gets done, one process at a time, so the organisation runs on reliable systems instead of paper, spreadsheets and workarounds. Auris Nexus helps organisations across South Africa plan that change and then implement it, in phases they can afford and their people can adopt.

Get a free assessment Chat on WhatsApp

What digital transformation covers

  • Business system modernisation: replacing or improving systems that hold the business back
  • Digital process transformation: moving paper, email and spreadsheet processes onto structured workflows
  • Technology implementation: building, configuring and rolling out the chosen solutions
  • Legacy system improvement: extending, integrating or gradually replacing older systems
  • Digital infrastructure planning: hosting, data, integrations, access control and security
  • Change support: training, documentation and adoption for the people who use the systems

Business problems it solves

  • Core processes still depend on paper, spreadsheets and email approvals
  • An older system works, but nobody can change or extend it safely
  • Departments use separate tools and re-capture the same data
  • Earlier technology projects were bought but never properly adopted
  • Leadership lacks a single, current view of operations

How we work

A practical path from current state to modern operations

Each phase is small enough to manage and delivers something the organisation can use.

01

Assess

We map current processes, systems, data and pain points with the people who do the work.

02

Prioritise

We identify which changes will remove the most friction or risk first, and which can wait.

03

Plan

04

Implement

We build, configure or integrate the systems for each phase, starting with the highest-value change.

05

Adopt

06

Improve

Digital Transformation FAQ

Straight answers on digital transformation.

For most organisations it means taking the processes that run on paper, spreadsheets and email, and moving them onto reliable systems one at a time, then connecting those systems so information is captured once and reported consistently. It does not need to be a large, single programme.

Start with the process that causes the most manual work, errors or risk today, and that a clear group of people own. A short technology consulting engagement can map your processes and systems and recommend a first phase.

Not necessarily. Many systems can be kept and integrated, extended or gradually replaced. We recommend replacing a system only when keeping it costs more, in time, risk or workarounds, than moving on.

It depends on the scope of each phase. Purpose-built business systems at Auris Nexus start from R30,000, and larger custom and enterprise platforms, including integrations and legacy modernisation, start from R50,000. A phased roadmap gives you a budget range for each step. See pricing.

We involve the people who do the work in discovery and design, release in phases so change is manageable, and provide training, documentation and support after launch.
