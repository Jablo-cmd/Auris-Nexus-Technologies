About Loyiso

Loyiso Ngcala is the Founder and Managing Director of Auris Nexus Technologies, a technology consulting and digital solutions company working with South African businesses on custom software, business automation, systems integration and practical AI adoption.

Loyiso writes Auris Nexus Insights to share direct, practical thinking on how growing organisations plan technology decisions, replace manual processes with proper systems, and adopt automation and AI without adding unnecessary complexity.

Areas of focus

  • Technology consulting and roadmapping
  • Custom software and business systems
  • Business process automation
  • Practical AI adoption
  • Digital transformation strategy

Company

Loyiso founded Auris Nexus Technologies (Pty) Ltd in July 2026. The company is registered with CIPC under registration number 2026/606690/07 and is based at 140 Linden Street, Sandown, Sandton, Gauteng 2196, South Africa. As Managing Director, Loyiso leads client engagements from the first consultation through delivery.

Auris Nexus projects

Auris Nexus projects include Funda360, a multi-tenant school management platform; the CIT Leave Management System; LOGIOS OS, a logistics operations platform in active development; Sebetsa, a workforce and operations platform; and the Pro Energy Solutions corporate website.

How Loyiso writes

Articles are written for owners and managers making technology decisions, not for developers. Each one aims to answer a practical question, such as whether to build or buy, what to automate first or what POPIA means for a new system, in plain language. Articles follow the Auris Nexus editorial policy, including how corrections are made.
