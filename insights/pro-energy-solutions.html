Pro Energy Solutions — Corporate website

A modern, brand-led corporate website for a solar energy company focused on trust, clarity and qualified enquiries.

Client overview

Pro Energy Solutions is a renewable energy provider specialising in solar installations and electrical services for residential and commercial customers. They required a professional online presence that reflected their technical expertise and environmental focus.

Project overview

Auris Nexus Technologies designed and delivered a modern corporate website showcasing services, case studies and clear contact pathways to increase trust and generate enquiries.

Business objectives

  • Present a credible, modern brand that appeals to residential and commercial clients.
  • Improve online visibility through SEO foundations and clear service pages.
  • Increase qualified leads via optimised contact flows and CTAs.

Challenges

Low brand consistency, limited content structure for services, and no clear conversion paths for commercial clients.

Solution delivered

We delivered a brand-focused design system, fast responsive templates, structured content for services and SEO-friendly markup to improve discoverability.

Key website features

  • Hero-focused service pages with clear trust signals
  • Prominent CTAs and contact pathways
  • Performance optimised images and lazy-loading
  • Accessible markup and focus states

Responsive design

The site was built mobile-first with carefully tested breakpoints to ensure an excellent experience on phones, tablets and desktops.

SEO foundation

Page metadata, semantic headings, descriptive image alt text and sitemap inclusion to support organic discovery.

Performance optimisation

Optimised image assets, minimal JavaScript, and CSS delivered in a compact design system to reduce load times and improve Core Web Vitals.

Accessibility improvements

Clear colour contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation and visible focus outlines to improve usability for all visitors.

Business value delivered

The new website elevated brand trust, improved lead capture and provided a scalable foundation for future marketing.
