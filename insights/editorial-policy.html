Editorial Standards

Auris Nexus Technologies publishes technology, business, software, automation, AI and digital transformation insights for business decision-makers.

Authorship

Articles carry a visible byline where an individual contributor is identified, or Auris Nexus Technologies where the company is the responsible publisher.

Accuracy and sourcing

We aim to verify factual claims against reliable primary or authoritative sources where appropriate. Analysis and opinion are presented as analysis or opinion rather than as reported fact.

Corrections

When we identify a material factual error, we correct the article and, where appropriate, explain the correction. We do not change publication dates simply to make older articles appear new.

AI-assisted content

AI tools may assist with research, drafting, editing or formatting. Published material remains subject to human review, fact checking and editorial responsibility by Auris Nexus Technologies.
