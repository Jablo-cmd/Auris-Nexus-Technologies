# How AI assistants can improve business operations

**Short answer:** an AI assistant is useful when it is connected to your organisation's own information and systems, answers a defined set of questions or completes a defined set of tasks, and hands over to a person when it is unsure. Used this way, it reduces the time staff spend searching for information and handling routine requests.

## Internal assistants (for staff)

- **Policy and procedure questions:** "How many days' study leave do I get?" answered from the current HR policy, with a link to the source.
- **System guidance:** helping staff find the right screen or process in a business system.
- **Record summaries:** summarising a client's history or a case file before a meeting.
- **Drafting:** first drafts of routine emails, reports or job descriptions for a person to edit.

## Customer-facing assistants

- **Answering common questions** about services, hours, documents required or order status, using approved information only.
- **Collecting enquiry details** (what the customer needs, contact details) and passing a structured summary to the right person.
- **Out-of-hours triage**, with clear hand-over to a person on the next working day.

## What makes an assistant work

1. **A defined scope.** List the questions and tasks it should handle. Everything else is handed over.
2. **Grounding in your content.** It answers from your documents and systems, not from general internet knowledge, and shows where an answer came from.
3. **Access control.** It only sees the information the user is allowed to see.
4. **Human hand-over.** A clear route to a person, and a log of conversations for review.
5. **Ongoing maintenance.** When a policy changes, the assistant's source content must change too.

## Limitations and risks

- Assistants can give wrong answers with confidence. Keep them away from final decisions on money, employment, health or legal matters.
- Personal information must be handled in line with POPIA, including what is sent to external AI services.
- Usage-based costs grow with volume; estimate them.
- An assistant cannot fix poor or outdated source information.
