# Digital transformation challenges facing African SMEs

**Short answer:** most small and medium-sized businesses in Africa do not fail at digital transformation because of technology. They struggle with unreliable infrastructure, limited budgets, skills gaps, processes that were never written down, and systems that were bought but not adopted. Phased, practical projects that fit local conditions get further than large programmes.

## 1. Connectivity and power

Internet access and electricity can be unreliable, depending on the location.
**What helps:** cloud systems that tolerate interruptions, mobile-friendly interfaces, offline-capable apps for field staff where needed, and choosing hosting with the users' location in mind.

## 2. Budget and cash flow

Large up-front software investments are hard to justify for a smaller business.
**What helps:** phasing work so each phase pays back before the next starts; preferring configuration of existing tools where they fit; budgeting for running costs, not only the build.

## 3. Processes that live in people's heads

Many SMEs run on the knowledge of a few long-serving staff. Digitising that requires writing it down first.
**What helps:** process mapping with the people who do the work before any software is chosen.

## 4. Skills and adoption

New systems fail when staff are not trained or not involved.
**What helps:** involving users in design, releasing in small steps, training, and a named internal owner.

## 5. Fragmented tools

Businesses often accumulate several separate apps, spreadsheets and messaging groups.
**What helps:** deciding which system is the source of truth for each type of information, and integrating the rest.

## 6. Data protection across borders

Many African countries have their own data-protection laws, such as South Africa's POPIA, Kenya's Data Protection Act and Nigeria's Data Protection Act. Requirements differ.
**What helps:** knowing where data is stored and processed, and getting local advice when operating across borders. *[Editor: verify each law named against its official source before publishing.]*

## 7. Payments and local integrations

Local payment methods, including mobile money in several markets, may need integrating.
**What helps:** checking integration options early in the design.
