Healthcare administration

Healthcare Administration Software for Practices and Clinics

Auris Nexus designs administration software for private practices, clinics, care providers and health NGOs in South Africa. Bookings, patient administration, staff rosters, compliance and reporting can run from one system, with access control designed for POPIA from the first day.

Discuss your requirements Book a free consultation
The operational challenge

Clinical time is lost to administration.

Practices and care providers handle bookings, patient paperwork, staff rosters and compliance documents alongside patient care. When those tasks run on paper files, phone calls and spreadsheets, admin staff are stretched, mistakes creep in, and sensitive information is harder to protect.

  • Appointments are managed by phone and a paper or spreadsheet diary
  • Patient forms and consent documents are kept as paper files
  • Rosters and leave for nurses and carers are rebuilt every week
  • Practitioner registrations and training records are tracked by memory
  • Consumable stock runs out before anyone notices
What we can build for healthcare

Administration and operations, not clinical decision-making.

We focus on the work around patient care. Each module can be built on its own or as part of one system, and connected to the practice or billing software you already use where the vendor allows.

Bookings and reminders

Online booking, appointment reminders and a shared calendar across practitioners and rooms, reducing no-shows and front-desk phone time.

Patient administration

Structured registration, contact details, consent forms and uploaded documents in place of paper files, with access limited by role.

Staff rostering and leave

Shift rosters, on-call schedules and leave for nurses, carers and admin staff, with clear approval steps and attendance records.

Credential and compliance tracking

Practitioner registrations, training, inspections and policies on record, with reminders well before anything expires.

Consumables and equipment

Stock levels per site, controlled receipt and issue of consumables, and an equipment register with service dates.

Management reporting

Dashboards on appointments, utilisation, staffing and outstanding admin tasks, for a single practice or several sites.

Who we build for

  • Private GP, dental and specialist practices
  • Multi-practitioner clinics and practice groups
  • Home-based care and nursing agencies with staff in the field
  • Health NGOs and community health programmes
  • Occupational health and wellness providers

What we do and don't do

  • We build administration and operations systems for healthcare organisations
  • We do not build clinical decision-support or diagnostic software
  • We work alongside existing practice management and medical aid billing systems rather than replacing them
  • Health information is special personal information under POPIA, and the design treats it that way
  • Every engagement starts with consulting, so requirements are clear before anything is built

Our relevant experience

Auris Nexus has not yet delivered a healthcare-specific system. Our closest work is in HR and workforce platforms: Sebetsa covers rostering, attendance, leave, compliance and inventory with role-based access, and the CIT Leave Management System handles leave approvals and HR records.

How we work

Requirements first, especially where patient data is involved.

A healthcare engagement begins with a consulting phase: we map the administrative workflow with your practice manager and staff, identify which information is sensitive and who needs it, and agree hosting, retention and access rules before development starts. The system is then delivered in phases, starting with the task that costs your team the most time.

Healthcare software FAQ

Questions practices and providers ask us.
