School Management Systems, LMS & School Apps in South Africa

Auris Nexus builds school management systems, learning management systems (LMS) and school apps for independent schools, school groups, colleges and training providers in Johannesburg and across South Africa. Admissions, learner records, fees, parent communication and learning, in one system built around how your school works.

What we build for schools

Every system a school runs on, connected.

Start with the module that hurts most today and add the rest over time. Everything shares one set of learner, parent and staff records.

School management system

A school management system (student information system) that holds learner records, staff, classes and admissions in one place, with access scoped for principals, admin, finance and teachers.

Learning management system (LMS)

An LMS for online and blended learning: course content, assignments, quizzes, marking and progress tracking that learners and teachers can use from any device.

Online admissions and enrolment

Online application forms with document uploads, waiting lists and an approval workflow, so admissions stop living in inboxes and paper files.

School fees management

Fee billing, payment tracking, statements and arrears visibility, so the bursar sees who has paid without reconciling spreadsheets.

Parent portal and school app

A parent portal or mobile school app for notices, reports, fee statements and messages, connected to the same records the school runs on.

Attendance, timetables and reports

Registers, timetabling, assessments and report cards built into the same system, instead of separate tools that never agree.

Who we build for

  • Independent and private schools
  • School groups running more than one campus
  • Private colleges and TVET colleges
  • Training providers, academies and tutoring centres
  • Early childhood development (ECD) centres and preschools

Why schools choose a custom system

  • Built around your school's processes, not a generic template
  • One platform for every campus, with each school's data kept separate
  • Learner data protected by role-based access and two-factor authentication
  • Works on phones for parents, teachers and admin staff
  • Connects to the tools you already use, such as accounting and payments
