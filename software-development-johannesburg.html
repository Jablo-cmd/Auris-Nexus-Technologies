Custom Software Development in Johannesburg

Auris Nexus Technologies is a software development company based in Sandton, Johannesburg. We build business systems around the way your organisation already works, instead of asking you to change your processes to fit off-the-shelf software. We map the process first, then design the smallest system that solves it properly.

Discuss your process See case studies

What we build for Johannesburg businesses

  • Business systems. CRM, HR and leave management, booking, inventory and membership platforms, with role-based security and reporting dashboards.
  • Workflow automation. Approvals, notifications, documents and data moving between the tools you already use, with an audit trail.
  • Integrations. Forms, email, CRM, accounting and HR systems connected so data is not captured twice.
  • Multi-tenant platforms. One system that several organisations, schools or teams run independently.
  • AI integration and mobile apps. Added where they save real time, not for the sake of it.

How we work

  • Discovery. We understand your goals, current systems and priorities, and map the process as it runs today.
  • Design. We shape the system around your workflow, roles and approvals.
  • Development. We build with quality, security and performance in mind, designed with POPIA principles.
  • Launch and support. We deploy, monitor and keep improving after go-live.
