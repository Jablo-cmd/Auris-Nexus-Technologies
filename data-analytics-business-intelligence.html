Data & Business Intelligence

Business Intelligence Solutions & Data Analytics

The numbers exist — in the CRM, the finance system, the spreadsheets — but pulling them together takes days, and by then they're out of date. We connect the sources, agree the metrics that matter, and build reporting dashboards that update themselves: business intelligence solutions for companies in Johannesburg and across South Africa.

Get a free assessment Ask a question

What we build

  • Management dashboards. The handful of numbers leadership actually watches, live and in one place.
  • Operational reporting. The day-to-day view each team needs to run their part of the business.
  • Automated reports. Board packs and weekly summaries that build and send themselves.
  • Data integration. Pull data together from the systems it's scattered across.

How we approach it

  • Start from the decisions you're trying to make, then work back to the data
  • Agree definitions up front so everyone trusts the same numbers
  • Automate the refresh so no one is rebuilding a spreadsheet every Monday
  • Keep it readable — a dashboard nobody understands is worse than none

Reporting is better when the system is right

Good BI usually follows a good business system. If your data is messy, we'll say so — and help fix the source.
