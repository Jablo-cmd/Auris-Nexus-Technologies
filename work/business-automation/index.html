03 — Business Automation

Less manual work. More operational flow.

Intelligent workflows, process optimisation, operational efficiency and business automation solutions, designed around how your teams really work.

Capabilities

What we automate.

Repetitive work tends to hide in approvals, admin and hand-offs. These are the areas we most often help teams streamline.

1

Approvals and sign-off

Requests, reviews and approvals that move through the right people in the right order, with a clear record of who decided what.

2

Admin and data capture

Forms, documents and repeated data entry replaced by structured capture that flows straight into the systems that need it.

3

Connected tools

Email, forms, spreadsheets and business systems joined together so information is entered once and stays consistent.

Our approach

Four steps we follow

Map the process

Understand how the work happens today: the steps, the people and the hand-offs.

Choose what to automate first

Start where manual effort and risk are highest, not where the technology is most exciting.

Build and connect

Implement the workflow and join it to your existing tools, with people kept in the loop where judgement matters.

Measure and refine

Check it works in daily use and adjust as the business changes.

Where this shows up in our products. Workflow is already part of the platforms we build: attendance, scheduling, leave and task workflows in Sebetsa, and dispatch, trip and delivery workflows in LOGIOS OS. We do not yet publish a standalone business-automation case study, so this page describes our capabilities and approach rather than client results.
