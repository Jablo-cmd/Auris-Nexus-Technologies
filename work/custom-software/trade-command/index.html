Trade Command

A private, mobile-first console for running up to 40 trading accounts from one screen: one master signal, individually validated and risk-sized for every account before anything is authorised.

Read the full case studyMore Custom Software Development work
EngagementClient project
DisciplineCustom Software Development
StatusBuilt & tested

The opportunity

Placing the same trade across dozens of accounts by hand invites errors, because every account has its own balance, limits and rules.

What we shaped

One master signal that is checked against each account separately, with nothing released until the owner approves it.

What we delivered

A phone-first web app with account groups, risk sizing, staged entries, take-profit control, order tracking and an audit trail that only ever grows.

Current status: built and tested. Broker connectivity is being brought online in stages and is not described here as live.

Key capabilities

  • Capacity for up to 40 accounts, enforced at every layer
  • Account groups with their own offsets and approval rules
  • A single signal validated for each account
  • Risk sized from each server’s instrument specification
  • Laddered entries with stop-loss and take-profit recalculated from fills
  • Emergency disable and order reconciliation
  • Credentials redacted in the audit log
