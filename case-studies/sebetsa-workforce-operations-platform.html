Sebetsa — workforce and operations platform

An enterprise platform designed to connect people, sites, tasks, compliance and operational reporting in one controlled workspace.

The problem

Operational businesses need more than an employee list. They need to know who is working, where work is happening, what tasks are open, which assets are involved and where compliance or procurement requires attention.

The platform

Sebetsa brings workforce and site operations into a multi-tenant system with role-aware access and a shared operational record.

Core areas

  • Employee and workforce management
  • Site operations and attendance
  • Scheduling and task management
  • Compliance and document control
  • Assets and inventory
  • Procurement and reporting

Engineering approach

The platform is designed as modular enterprise software so operational domains can evolve without fragmenting the organisation's data. Security and role boundaries are treated as part of the data architecture rather than only UI behaviour.

What it enables

Management gains a more complete operational picture while teams work from shared records, defined workflows and controlled access.

Focus: Workforce intelligence and operations
