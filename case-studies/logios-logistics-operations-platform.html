LOGIOS OS — logistics operations platform

A modular, multi-tenant workspace for logistics scheduling, jobs, incidents, fleet, drivers, customers, compliance and reporting.

The operational problem

Logistics teams can end up coordinating jobs, vehicles, drivers, customers and incidents through calls, spreadsheets and message threads. The result is fragmented information and weak operational visibility.

The platform approach

LOGIOS OS is designed around a multi-tenant foundation with shared identity, role controls and auditing. Operational domains are delivered as modules so the platform can expand without creating another disconnected system.

Current capabilities

  • Scheduling and job management
  • Incident logging and tracking
  • Fleet and driver management
  • Customer records
  • Compliance and operational reporting
  • Tenant isolation and role-based access

Engineering

The web application uses React, TypeScript, Vite, Supabase and PostgreSQL. Row-level security scopes data access to the organisation and user permissions at the data layer.

Status

LOGIOS OS remains in active development. Dispatch, trips, deliveries and fuel and expenses are planned as additional operational modules.
