LOGIOS OS — logistics operations platform

In active development Auris Nexus product Operations software Logistics Custom software

Bringing logistics operations — scheduling, jobs, incidents, fleet, drivers, customers and compliance — into one controlled, role-aware workspace instead of separate tools and message threads.

The challenge

Logistics operators coordinate jobs, vehicles, drivers, customers and incidents across phone calls, spreadsheets and separate apps. Nothing is joined up, accountability is hard to trace, and there's no single operational picture.

The approach

Design a modular operations platform on a multi-tenant foundation: shared identity, roles and audit from day one, with each operational domain built as a module that can be switched on as it's ready.

The solution

A web application where an organisation manages scheduling, jobs, incidents, a fleet register, drivers, customers, compliance and reporting from one workspace, with role-based access and a system-status view. Dispatch, trips, deliveries and fuel & expenses are in development.

Key capabilities

  • Multi-tenant architecture — each operator is an isolated tenant with its own users
  • Role-based access — scoped to the user's role, e.g. system administrator
  • Scheduling and job management
  • Incident logging and tracking
  • Fleet register and driver management
  • Customer records
  • Compliance module and operational reporting
  • Modular design — operational domains enabled as they're delivered

Technical approach

LOGIOS is a single-page application backed by a managed PostgreSQL database. Tenant isolation and per-role access are enforced at the data layer with row-level security, so every query is scoped to the organisation and the user's permissions. The module structure keeps each operational domain independent.

React TypeScript Vite Supabase PostgreSQL Row-level security Multi-tenant SaaS

What Auris Nexus did

Product and platform design, the multi-tenant data model, role and permission design, and full-stack engineering of the workspace and its modules. Development is ongoing.

What it enables

A single operational picture for a logistics business, traceable actions through roles and auditing, and a foundation that takes on new operational domains as modules rather than new systems.
